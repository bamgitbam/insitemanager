Sawyer

See the field. Keep the footage close.

Sawyer is a mobile-focused application for camera live view and local field workflows. It supports compatible action-camera and saved RTSP paths, while a portable Raspberry Pi camera node is being developed for independent recording and later media retrieval.

For action-camera users, trail riders, campers and field technicians working on private networks.

  • Live view for compatible camera paths
  • Saved cameras on private RTSP networks
  • Standalone Pi recording foundation in development

What's working

Field viewing on a local network

Sawyer supports compatible action-camera workflows and saved private-network RTSP sources on Android. Galaxy S21 Live and Field View frames were observed from an operator-run Pi camera stream.

Platform direction

Live viewing now. Independent capture next.

Sawyer connects to supported cameras on local networks and is growing toward a portable camera node that can record independently of a connected phone. Each transport and camera family is verified on its own terms.

Explore qualification milestones →

Established viewing paths

Galaxy S21 viewing of a Pi H.264 stream and the named GoPro HERO3+ direct-live path have device-specific evidence, not universal compatibility.

Separate recording milestones

Phone-owned RTSP capture, resilient GoPro media transfer, unattended Pi segments and retention, and later mobile review require further implementation or field proof.
